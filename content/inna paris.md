Of writing being a paradigm shift and some important things that shift enables:

> In a sense, the transcendent is born at that point, for meaning now lives without a speaker or a listener. It also makes possible solemnity without emphasis, and respect for content rather than for context.

Compare with the nature of LLMs – that the predictive and probabilistic nature of LLMs *is* thought or close enough to it in many circumstances that matter.
