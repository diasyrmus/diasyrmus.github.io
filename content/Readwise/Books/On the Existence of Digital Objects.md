- Part I, “Object,” paves the path to an understanding of the emergence of digital objects in the history of philosophy. I don’t mean by this that digital objects had already been anticipated by traditional metaphysics but rather that as digital objects have emerged as a consequence of historical and technological development, so have they also inherited certain metaphysical presuppositions.
